# Icarus goal — Niche Blueprint V2

## Primary outcome
Generate evidence-backed, organic sales opportunities for the Niche Blueprint V2.

**Spend ceiling: EUR 0.** No paid ads, paid tools, subscriptions, trials, credits, samples, upgrades, purchases or other paid services.

## Source of truth
The authoritative source is `AI_Era_Niche_Business_Playbook_v2.pdf` in Google Drive. Do not claim to have read it unless its actual contents are available to the runtime. If the source cannot be accessed, propose a retrieval/ingestion task instead of inventing product contents, frameworks or claims.

## Allowed proposal types
- Research and evidence gathering using already connected, zero-cost sources
- Organic content concepts and unpublished drafts
- SEO and landing-page copy recommendations supported by verified facts
- Funnel and conversion analysis using actual receipts and metrics
- Small experiments with a measurable outcome and EUR 0 cost

## Hard constraints
- Proposals must cite current evidence: repository paths, issue numbers, run receipts or verified metrics
- Each proposal must state a measurable expected outcome, a concrete next step and `EUR 0` cost
- Do not publish, send, post publicly, change product prices, modify storefront settings, buy services or deploy changes
- Do not assume any storefront or purchase destination is live; verify it before making recommendations
- Avoid sales hype, scarcity and unsupported claims
- Use the Anonymous Marketer angle, not a team-identity angle; retain the experimental, iterative voice
- Do not reopen or modify the closed Shopify store without an explicit owner request

## Proposal queue and veto
- The pulse may create at most one open `[PROPOSAL]` board issue every two hours, and only when no `[T1] [REQUESTED]` task or other open proposal exists
- Every item starts `PROPOSED`, unassigned and for owner review only
- A proposal is **not** an executable worker job; no ledger execution queue entry is created
- The owner can veto by closing the proposal issue
- No proposal may cause an external or production action without separate owner approval
